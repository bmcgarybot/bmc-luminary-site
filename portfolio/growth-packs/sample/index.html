Typical fields

The spreadsheet is meant to answer “who first?”

Typical fields include prospect name, market, website, public business email/phone when available, source, fit reason, priority, likely need, outreach angle, and research notes. Premium work adds deeper market/competitor strategy rather than simply more rows.
