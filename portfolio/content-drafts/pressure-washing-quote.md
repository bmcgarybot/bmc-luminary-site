# What should you include in a pressure-washing quote request?

Tell the provider what needs cleaning, roughly how large it is, where it is, and what you want the finished job to include. Add clear photos if you have them. Those details give the provider a better starting point than a request that says only “How much to pressure wash?”

There isn't a useful single price we can promise for every driveway, storefront or house in Arizona. Ask for a quote for your actual property and compare the scope as well as the total.

## Start with the surface and the problem

Describe the area in ordinary language: a driveway, front walkway, shop entrance, loading area, patio or building exterior. Say what you want addressed, such as general buildup or a particular stain. Mention the material if you know it; otherwise say you are unsure.

Give approximate dimensions or the area you want included. If you're requesting a quote for a house, distinguish the exterior surfaces to be cleaned from the home's interior floor area. Photos can help explain the job, but the provider may still need an inspection or more information before giving a firm price.

## Explain access and timing

Include the town or ZIP code, your preferred dates and any access restrictions. For a business property, mention opening hours, customer foot traffic and areas that need to stay accessible. Ask what arrangements the provider needs for water, equipment access and cleanup.

Tell the provider about known damage or delicate areas rather than assuming that every surface should receive the same treatment. Ask the provider to explain the method proposed for your material and condition. A general question list isn't a substitute for that assessment.

## Ask what the written price includes

Before accepting, ask for a scope that identifies:

- The surfaces and approximate area included.
- Any spot treatment or preparation included in the price.
- Access, setup or minimum-service charges.
- Items excluded and how extra work would be approved.
- The expected schedule, payment timing and cancellation terms.

If two quotes differ, check whether they include the same surfaces and tasks. A lower total may cover less work. Ask about insurance and any credentials relevant to the actual work; BMC does not establish a provider's suitability simply by finding its website.

## A request you can copy

“I need a quote for [surface] at a [home/business] in [town/ZIP]. The area is approximately [dimensions]. I want [scope/problem] addressed. My preferred dates are [dates]. Access is [details], and [known damage or restrictions] may affect the work. Please explain the proposed method, what your price includes, any exclusions and whether you need to inspect first.”

## Common questions

### How much should pressure washing a driveway cost?

Request a price for the actual driveway, its condition and the agreed work. We don't have a verified local price range to publish here. Ask whether the quote includes preparation, spot treatment, edges or adjoining walkways if those matter to you.

### Is a quote for a 2,000-square-foot house enough information?

Explain which exterior areas you want cleaned. The living-area number alone may not describe the cleaning scope. Photos, surface information and access details give the provider more useful context.

### Can BMC guarantee someone can take my job?

No. A request starts a review and matching process. Provider coverage and acceptance must be confirmed for your service and location. Do not treat a request acknowledgment as a confirmed appointment.
