How it works

More than a form that forwards an email.

BMC is designed to manage a request from intake through provider response and outcome tracking.

1. Structured intake

Market, scope, timing, contact details and service-specific information are captured in a common format.

2. Qualification

The workflow validates required fields, classifies the service, scores urgency and sends ambiguous cases to an exception queue.

3. Fit-based matching

Only participating providers matching the service and declared territory are eligible. Capacity, job fit and provider status can further narrow routing.

4. Accept or decline

A provider receives a secure request link and can accept or decline. Declined or expired offers can move to the next eligible provider.

5. Follow-through

BMC tracks whether the customer was contacted, an estimate was sent, the work was won or lost, and whether another follow-up is needed.

6. Recurring service

For repeat categories, the same operating system can support maintenance schedules, reorders, recurring routes and preferred-provider relationships.
