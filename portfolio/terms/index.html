Terms

Clear terms for BMC services and research products.

These terms apply to BMC Service Network and BMC Growth Intelligence unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

BMC's role

BMC Luminary Ventures LLC operates service-request, sourcing, referral, coordination, research, and concierge services. Unless BMC expressly agrees otherwise in writing, independent providers—not BMC—perform third-party field work.

Growth Intelligence research

BMC Growth Intelligence identifies commercial businesses that match the service, market, and account criteria a customer provides. A qualified commercial prospect is a researched fit; it does not mean that business has asked for the service, agreed to a meeting, or promised to buy.

Qualification guarantee

When a paid Growth offer includes the BMC qualification guarantee, BMC will replace a delivered prospect that was outside the agreed territory, inactive when delivered, the wrong business or property type, a duplicate previously delivered by BMC to the same customer, or missing the promised contact route. BMC does not guarantee replies, estimates, sales, revenue, or any other business outcome.

Research sources and contact information

Research may use public business websites, public records, directories, registries, public contact information, and other publicly available sources. Customers are responsible for how they use delivered information and for following applicable outreach, privacy, and marketing rules.

Payments and fulfillment

Prices, deliverables, and any offer-specific terms are shown before purchase. BMC may pause fulfillment when the requested market or criteria cannot be completed honestly without lowering the published qualification standard, and will contact the customer about an appropriate resolution.

Independent providers

Participating Service Network providers are independent businesses. Provider availability, licensing, insurance, estimates, contracts, warranties, scheduling, workmanship, and final pricing remain the responsibility of the provider and customer unless a separate written BMC agreement says otherwise.

No guarantee of service-network outcome

Submitting a service request does not guarantee provider availability, a specific price, a particular response time, or successful completion. BMC may decline, reroute, or stop working a request when a suitable participating provider is unavailable.

Provider network

Application to the BMC network does not guarantee acceptance, exclusivity, opportunity volume, or revenue. BMC may pause or remove providers based on fit, responsiveness, service area, quality history, complaints, or operational requirements.
