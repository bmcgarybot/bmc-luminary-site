# Growth research territories and offer sizes

Providers select a main city and check the additional Arizona cities where they accept work. Travel notes provide context; they never silently add cities. The selected set is saved through email verification, sampling, checkout and delivery. Existing samples retain their original city. Nevada stays separate.

The 21 market labels remain their actual researched geography. Cordes Lakes is not silently renamed Cordes Junction. Unlisted towns go into travel/custom-scope notes until their research and coverage can be confirmed.

## Current implemented offer behavior

- Five free researched prospects when the chosen cities can support five qualified, fairly rotated records.
- A 100-total-prospect pack for the existing $499 price only when the chosen territory clears the unique inventory reserve and saved-five rechecks. The original five are included in the 100.
- A visible “Ask about a smaller territory pack” on-site request form when the 100-pack isn't offered. It saves one review task per submission and creates no order or payment entitlement. Confirm available quantity, price, scope and delivery terms before payment.
- If even five aren't available, save the request for research review. Do not manufacture a sample or claim an unavailable provider/customer.

No smaller paid product, quantity or checkout price is activated by this change. A 20- or 50-prospect offer may be appropriate after the actual territory research, buyer exclusions, reuse rules and delivery cost are known. Product size is not the minimum business count a town must have to deserve service.

## Examples

A provider may choose Sedona + Cottonwood + Camp Verde, or Flagstaff + Cottonwood + Camp Verde, if those match its actual service area. The selector validates each record against its own city and deduplicates the combined business names before counting capacity. Adding city counts is not proof of a saleable regional pack. Winslow is included only if selected.

A small Ash Fork or rural custom territory can remain a smaller request/research offer. Do not widen it to Prescott or Flagstaff merely to make a headline count.

## Invariants

- Replaying a request doesn't change its saved cities or rotate the original five.
- Changing the primary city or adding an overlapping city doesn't unlock another free sample for the same business.
- Source, current-record, fit, suppression, duplicate, exposure and payment-event checks remain in place.
- Checkout is rechecked on initial offer, replay and nurture; an underfilled or newly suppressed/stale pool loses the link.
- Paid CSV rows keep actual city labels and all remain inside the agreed selected set.
- Owner preview remains read-only. It cannot create samples, emails, orders or payments.
- All outbound campaigns remain held until the separate owner review/launch gate is satisfied.
