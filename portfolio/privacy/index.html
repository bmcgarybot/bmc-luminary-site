Privacy

How BMC uses service-request information

This notice applies to BMC Service Network requests, partner applications, research products, and related operations.

Information submitted

We may collect contact details, business or property information, service scope, timing, market, referral/source information, and other details you choose to provide through a request or partner application.

How it is used

BMC uses submitted information to classify requests, coordinate with participating providers, respond to inquiries, operate the provider network, track service outcomes, improve the portfolio, prevent abuse, and maintain business records.

Provider sharing

When you request a local service, relevant request information may be shared with one or more participating independent providers so they can evaluate, accept, quote, schedule, or perform the requested work.

Outreach

BMC may contact publicly listed businesses about relevant B2B services or provider opportunities. Businesses can ask to stop outreach at any time, and suppression requests are recorded.

Retention and security

Operational records may be retained as reasonably needed for service coordination, quality tracking, compliance, accounting, dispute handling, and business analysis. BMC uses reasonable technical controls but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to contact@bmcservicenetwork.com.
