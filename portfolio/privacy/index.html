Privacy

How BMC uses information you provide.

This notice applies to BMC Service Network requests, provider applications, Growth Intelligence research products, and related operations.

Information submitted

We may collect contact details, business or property information, service scope, market, preferred customer types, timing, referral or campaign information, and other details you choose to provide.

How it is used

BMC uses submitted information to respond to requests, deliver research products, coordinate service workflows, process and connect paid orders, track outcomes, prevent abuse, improve BMC services, and maintain business and accounting records.

Public business research

BMC Growth Intelligence may organize publicly available business information such as company names, public business contact routes, websites, public records, and source references to create commercial prospect research. BMC does not intentionally present private personal contact information as public business contact data.

Provider sharing

When you request a local service, relevant request information may be shared with one or more participating independent providers so they can evaluate, accept, quote, schedule, or perform the requested work.

Payments

Payment processing is handled through BMC's payment provider. BMC stores operational payment references and order status needed to connect a payment with the correct request or research order.

Business outreach

BMC may contact publicly listed businesses about relevant B2B services or provider opportunities. Businesses can ask to stop outreach at any time, and suppression requests are recorded so future outreach can be blocked.

Retention and security

Operational records may be retained as reasonably needed for service coordination, research fulfillment, quality tracking, compliance, accounting, dispute handling, and business analysis. BMC uses reasonable technical controls but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to contact@bmcservicenetwork.com.
