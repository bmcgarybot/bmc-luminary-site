Provider Pilot Terms

Simple pilot. No surprise billing.

These terms explain how BMC Luminary Ventures LLC tests local service-request routing with participating providers before any paid lead, subscription, territory, or referral arrangement begins.

What the pilot is

BMC may offer selected providers one or more qualified service requests at no charge during a pilot. Pilot placement does not guarantee lead volume, exclusivity, revenue, or future participation.

Future paid terms

No paid lead fee, territory subscription, referral fee, or other provider charge becomes active merely because you apply or accept a free pilot lead. BMC will present the applicable pricing and terms separately before any paid arrangement begins.

Independent provider

The provider remains an independent business and is responsible for its own pricing, scheduling, contracts, taxes, employees, equipment, permits, licensing, insurance, workmanship, safety, warranties, and legal obligations.

Customer relationship

BMC may qualify and route the request, but the provider is responsible for communicating its own scope, quote, service terms, and job-specific obligations directly to the customer unless BMC states otherwise in writing.

Licensing & insurance

Providers must maintain any licensing, registrations, permits, insurance, or certifications required for the work they perform. BMC may request verification and may pause routing when credentials are unclear or expired.

Lead information

Customer information supplied through BMC may be used only to evaluate and respond to the related service request. It should not be sold, redistributed, or added to unrelated marketing lists without the customer's permission.

Response & rerouting

Providers should accept or decline promptly. If a provider declines, does not respond within the offer window, or cannot service the request, BMC may route it to another provider.

Outcome reporting

Providers participating in the pilot should update whether the customer was contacted, an estimate was provided, and the job was won or lost. This helps BMC improve routing quality and future provider fit.

No endorsement or guarantee

Participation in the network is not an endorsement, employment relationship, franchise, partnership, or guarantee of provider quality or customer payment. BMC may pause or end participation when quality, response, compliance, or customer-experience concerns arise.

Disputes & refunds

During the free pilot there is no lead fee to refund. Any future paid program will have its own written billing, dispute, cancellation, and refund terms before activation.

Pilot acceptance

Submitting the BMC provider application indicates interest in the network. A provider becomes part of the active pilot only after BMC confirms acceptance. Accepting a routed pilot request confirms agreement to these pilot terms for that request.
