Nevada research terms

The Nevada research pilot

BMC Luminary Ventures LLC researches service businesses and commercial prospects. Submitting a request does not book work, enroll a provider or create an employment placement. Requests and payment remain closed until the pilot is approved.

Before purchase

BMC confirms the city, scope, deliverables, price and delivery terms before offering payment. The household research price shown on the preparation page is proposed. No household research fee includes the cost of third-party service work.

Independent decisions

Business prospects have not requested your services. Service businesses have not agreed to availability or participation merely because they appear in research. Confirm current service area, scope, credentials, insurance, screening, price and contract directly. BMC research does not guarantee those items, replies, sales or income.

Household employees and personal care

Nanny, household-employee placement and personal-care requests are not accepted through this pilot. BMC does not claim a Nevada employment-agency license or a Nevada office.

Research quality and problems

A current website or license record alone is not proof a business is operating, available or appropriately licensed. Research will identify its sources and relevant limitations. Contact contact@bmcservicenetwork.com about an error, inability to deliver or refund request. Offer-specific delivery and refund terms must be shown before any purchase.
