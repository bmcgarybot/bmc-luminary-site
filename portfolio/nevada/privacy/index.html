Nevada privacy

Information you submit

BMC Luminary Ventures LLC uses your email, chosen city, service, business name when supplied, consent and request details to review and reply about Nevada research. Please do not include children’s information, personal-care details, access codes, financial data or your exact home address.

Sharing and outreach

A research request does not authorize BMC to send your household details to service businesses. If sharing is needed later, BMC will ask first. BMC does not build private affluent-household contact lists. Businesses can ask to stop commercial outreach.

Research and payment records

Business research uses public company information and source references. BMC may retain request, consent, accounting and abuse-prevention records as reasonably needed. Payments, when an offer opens, are handled through a payment provider; BMC does not collect your card number in this form.

Contact and choices

For access, correction, deletion or a request to stop contact, email contact@bmcservicenetwork.com. Required accounting or dispute records may need to be retained.
