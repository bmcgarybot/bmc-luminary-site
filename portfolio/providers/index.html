Questions

Before you join.

Do I pay to join?

No. There is no listing or membership fee to join the pilot.

Do I have to take every job?

No. Accept the jobs that fit and pass on the ones that do not.

When would I pay BMC?

Not just for joining. Selected pilot opportunities may be free or reduced. Any later lead fee or territory price will be shown separately before it applies.

Does BMC guarantee jobs?

No. BMC cannot promise a certain number of requests or booked jobs. The pilot exists to prove whether the channel is useful before paid terms begin.
